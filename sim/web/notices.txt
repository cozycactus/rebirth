The hand model (the file handmodel.js)
---------------------------------------

The hands of Godot XR Tools, https://github.com/GodotVR/godot-xr-tools,
commit 6fc5361db295c12b5e51ee5b9fee09751a4458e7, folder
addons/godot-xr-tools/hands: model/Hand_Nails_L.gltf, model/Hand_Nails_R.gltf,
textures/caucasian_realistic_baseColor.png, textures/hands_normal.png and
textures/hands_occlusionRoughnessMetallic.png. handmodel.js holds their mesh
and skeleton and the textures as JPEG, converted by
sim/vendor/build-handmodel.py.
