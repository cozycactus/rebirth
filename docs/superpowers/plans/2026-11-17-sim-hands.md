# Hands on the Simulator Page — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Two realistic 3D hands over the simulator's drawn panel perform every finger action of the artist's score, with a toggle, checks in `sim/check.sh`, and a measured frame cost.

**Architecture:** A vendored three.js bundle and an embedded hand model; `handrig.js` builds, poses and fits one hand (Node and browser); `handplan.js` turns `show.actions` into per-hand keyframes; a driver in `handrig.js` samples them into poses; `handlayer.js` draws them in a WebGL canvas over `#device`; `app.js` loads it lazily and owns the toggle.

**Tech Stack:** classic-script JavaScript (no modules), three.js 0.187.0 bundled with esbuild 0.25.0, Node for checks (`node:assert`), Python 3 and macOS `sips` for the asset build, headless Chrome through puppeteer-core 25.16.0 for local verification only.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-17-sim-hands-design.md` (read it first; this plan does not repeat its reasons).

**Branch:** `claude/sim-hands` (from `origin/master`). The worktree holds an uncommitted throwaway prototype (`sim/web/handsgl.js`, `sim/web/handmesh.js`, `sim/web/three.js`, edits in `sim/web/index.html` and `sim/web/app.js`); Task 1 removes it. Its code, kept for reference (camera, lights, skin shader, forearm, pose tables), is `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-17-sim-hands-prototype.js`. `sim/emsdk` is an untracked symlink: never `git add -A`; add files by name.

## Global Constraints

- Classic scripts only, no `import`, no `fetch`, no workers: the page must work from `file://`.
- `sim/web/index.html` keeps exactly its eight `<script src="…">` tags (`sim/publish.sh` fails otherwise). The hands' scripts are loaded from `app.js`.
- New page files are named `[a-z]+.js` (plus `notices.txt`) and are listed in `PAGE_FILES` in `sim/publish.sh`.
- Global names: `THREE`, `HAND_MODEL`, `HandRig`, `HandPlan`, `HandLayer`. Never `Hands` or `hands` (taken).
- Each new script ends with the repository's export idiom (`sim/web/artist.js:1459-1461`): `module.exports = api` under Node, else `root.X = api`. Dependencies: under Node a script `require`s what it needs (`./three.js`, `./handmodel.js`, `./handplan.js`; `three.js` sets `globalThis.THREE`); in the browser it reads `root.THREE`, `root.HAND_MODEL`, `root.HandPlan` at call time, not at load time.
- Time: every `t`, duration and window in the hands' code is **device microseconds**, as `show.actions[i].t` (`artist.js:535-536`: `wait(ms)` adds `ms × 1000`). Names of such values end in `Us` where they are not `t`.
- Space: world units are page units, 1400 per side of the panel square = 300 mm (`UNITS_PER_MM = 1400 / 300`). Page point (u, v) is world (u, −v, 0); +z is up out of the plate. Heights stored in mm are named `…Mm`.
- Control keys (the identity used to pair a down with its up): `pad:<track>:<row>`, `scene:<row>`, `mute:<track>`, `step:<index>`, `play`, `knob:<enc>` (an `encsw` press and the `edge`s of that encoder share it). Tracks, rows and steps are 0-based, as in the actions (`artist.js:594-600`).
- Control tops above the plate (mm): pad 5.6, scene 4.6, mute 3.5, step 4.0, PLAY 5.0; an encoder knob is a cylinder 20 mm across and 14.5 mm tall.
- Eye: world (700, −2100, 4600); camera orientation identity (looks down −z, up +y), frustum shifted, never `lookAt`.
- Hand scale: the model × 0.92 (an 18.7 cm hand).
- A fingertip's **contact point** is picked per pose: the deepest vertex of the finger's last two segments along the contact direction (−z onto a top; toward the axis on a knob's side). Every "within N mm of the target" in tests and checks measures the contact point, never `rig.pad()` (the load-time pad, kept for Task 2's tests).
- `sim/web/artist.js`, `sim/test/artist_check.js` and the device's behaviour do not change.
- Every task: tests first and seen failing; `node sim/test/hands_unit.js` and (once wired) the whole `sim/check.sh` pass before the task's commit; one commit per task; files outside the task's list untouched.

## Review Focus

1. The toggle switched off and on while a step is held in the middle of a set: no control left ringed or pressed, and the hands come back with a finger already on the held step (Task 8, browser steps 5–6).
2. The artist stopped mid-gesture by a click on the panel (`takeOver`) or by "Выключить и включить", including while the hands' scripts are still loading: no exception, the hands leave within 300 ms, a later show plans from scratch (Task 8, browser step 7).
3. The page from `file://` with WebGL2 unavailable or a script failing to load: the ring works as today and the console has no uncaught error (Task 8, browser step 8).
4. A narrow window (the 900 px single-column layout) or a resize mid-set: the canvas follows `#device` and fingertips stay on the drawn controls (Task 8, browser step 9).
5. A hidden tab coming back mid-set (`MAX_STEP_S` clamps the device's catch-up): no finger shown on a control before its `down`, no replay of a backlog (Task 6 test `drawTime never reaches an unprocessed event`; Task 8, browser step 10).

### Task 3: Fitting — one fingertip, several, the pinch, clearance

**Files:**
- Modify: `sim/web/handrig.js`, `sim/test/hands_unit.js` (section "fit").

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: Task 2.
- Produces, on `Rig`:
  - `rig.armDir(target) -> THREE.Vector3`: from the shoulder (left `(350, −2000, 1633)`, right `(1050, −2000, 1633)`) to the target, flattened, then pitched 20° down.
  - `rig.contact(finger, toward) -> THREE.Vector3`: the contact point in the current pose; `toward` is a unit vector of the contact direction (default −z) or `{axis: point}` for a knob's side.
  - `rig.place(finger, target, dir)`: seeded from `point`; root rotation from `dir` and up `+z`, then translated so `rig.contact(finger)` equals `target` (re-pick and re-place, ≤ 3 rounds).
  - `rig.fit(targets, opts) -> { ok, residualMm }`: `targets` `{finger: Vector3}`; seeded from nearly straight fingers (never `relax`), with the root's yaw first set by the rigid (Procrustes) alignment of the seed's contact points with the targets when there are two or more, clamped to the yaw limits; then one damped-least-squares solve (λ = 0.1, numeric Jacobian, ≤ 40 iterations) over the root (translation, yaw, pitch; seeded from `opts.dir`) and per finger three angles (four fingers: spread and flex on proximal, flex on intermediate, distal = 2/3 of intermediate, metacarpal kept at the seed pose's value; thumb: spread and flex on metacarpal, one angle shared by proximal and distal), with limits (spread ±20°, flex 0–95°, thumb metacarpal flex −30–60°; yaw ±100° from the seed when `opts.freeYaw`, else ±30°) and a pull toward the seed pose of weight 0.05 per radian; the contact points are re-picked and the solve repeated (≤ 3 rounds); if a fingertip still hooks under (any of its vertices below the plate), the last segment's descent is capped and the solve repeated; `ok` iff every residual ≤ 1 mm.
  - `rig.pinch(enc, twistDeg) -> { ok, residualMm }`: the grip at twist 0 (thumb and index contact points on the knob's side, toward its axis) turned about the knob's axis by `twistDeg`; the rim has radius 10 mm, contacts at opposite points, 12 mm above the plate (the knob is 14.5 mm tall), the root seeded from `armDir` and free to roll up to 20° thumb side up; no vertex of either hand below the plate, as everywhere.
  - `rig.clear(active)`: fingers not in `active` curl toward `fist` (the thumb toward `lift`) by bisection until none of that finger's `CLEARANCE` vertices is `inside`; then, if a palm vertex is still inside, the root rises and `active` is refitted (≤ 3 rounds); a fixture triggers this path (or it is removed, with the reason in the report).
  - `rig.violations() -> [{vertex, mm}]`: `CLEARANCE` vertices `inside` a control or below the plate, deepest first (vertices of an active finger's last two segments inside its own target are exempt up to 1 mm deep; nothing is exempt below the plate).
  - `HandRig.POSES`: `point`, `relax`, `fist`, `lift` as in the prototype file's `POSES`, and `pinch` (the seed the pinch fit uses: thumb `[8.75, 4.5, 4.5]`, index `[0, 3, 26, 17]`, the others as `fist`; Task 3b).

  - `plan.turns`: `{ id, enc, tStart, tEnd, edges: [{t, dir}], push }`: edges of one encoder with gaps < `TURN_GAP_US` form one turn; `tEnd` = last edge + 20 000; `push` = the id of an `encsw` touch of the same encoder with `tDown ≤ tEnd + 200 000`, else null.

### Task 6: The driver — from keyframes to poses

**Files:**
- Modify: `sim/web/handrig.js` (`HandRig.Driver`, `HandRig.peakSpeed`), `sim/test/hands_unit.js` (section "drive").

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: `Plan` (Task 5), `Rig` (Tasks 2–3).
- Produces `new HandRig.Driver({left, right}, plan)` with:
  - `driver.pose(t, opts) -> { left: {away}, right: {away} }`: poses both rigs at `t`. Each keyframe's pose is fitted on first use (`place`, `fit` or `pinch` on the aims raised by `liftMm`) and cached by its geometry (side, kind, aims, `freeYaw`), so equal situations across shows reuse one fit (LRU of 4 000). Between keyframes the root (position lerp, rotation slerp) and the curls blend with minimum-jerk easing; a finger in contact is re-solved from the blended root only when its warm-start residual exceeds 1 mm (≤ 8 iterations). A pinch follows the knob angle (15° per detent counted from `turn.edges`) with a twist window of ±20°: inside a burst the fingers slide on the knob and the twist eases back toward the middle; in a gap between bursts ≥ 100 000 the hand lifts 3 mm and regrips. Then `rig.clear`. `opts.windowUs` (default 0): when > 0 the root is the average of 5 samples over `[t − windowUs/2, t + windowUs/2]`, contacts re-solved from it.
